How Autonomous Can GUI Agents Be? Understanding the Limitations of LLM-Based GUI Agents

An empirical study of 12 systems across 116 AndroidWorld tasks, with 1,392 executions, final failure annotations and reproducible offline analyses. Explore what GUI agents complete, why they fail, how errors evolve, and the gains and resource costs of continued execution.

围绕 12 个系统与 116 个 AndroidWorld 任务的实证研究，包含 1,392 次执行、最终失败标注与可复现离线分析。了解 GUI 智能体能完成什么、为何失败、错误如何演化，以及继续执行的收益与资源成本。
